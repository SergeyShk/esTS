    Cohesion statistics¶

    CohesionStats(source, sents_extractor=None, connectors=None, nlp=None)

    The cohesion statistics of a text in the manner of Coh-Metrix and of its Spanish adaptation Coh-Metrix-Esp: the overlap of the sentences, the givenness, the temporal cohesion and the connectors of a dictionary. The sentences are compared by lemmas. The data source is a text or a Doc object of spaCy, and it has to be annotated: a string is parsed with es_core_news_sm or with the pipeline passed in nlp, without the named entities, and a Doc must carry the parts of speech, which come from a morphologizer (or a tagger with an attribute_ruler), and the lemmas, which come from a lemmatizer.

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a source that is neither a string nor a Doc, an extractor of another type, a pipeline in nlp that is not a spaCy Language, connectors that are not a dictionary of phrases and pairs of strings;
    • SourceError - a source without words; a Doc without the parts of speech or the lemmas (a Doc of spacy.blank("es")); a string longer than the max_length of the pipeline or with a character without a UTF-8 form;
    • ParameterError - an unknown class or kind of a connector;
    • DatasetNotFoundError - a string without nlp when the model is not installed.

    The class extends the CohesionStats of the anyTS core with the Spanish layer. A Doc without sentence boundaries - from a pipeline without a parser - is split into sentences by the Spanish SentsExtractor. The features of the words come from the Universal Dependencies annotation, the temporal cohesion compares the tense and the mood of the verbs, and the connectors are found by SpanishConnectorMatcher over the dictionary of load_connectors. The print_stats table takes the descriptions of COHESION_STATS_DESC of ests.constants.

    The max_length of the pipeline is a million characters by default: split a longer text into parts, or raise max_length on a pipeline of your own and pass it in nlp.

    The words are classified by the Universal Dependencies annotation:

    Notion Universal Dependencies
    Nouns NOUN, PROPN
    Pronouns PRON and the determiners that point at something: possessive (Poss=Yes), demonstrative or personal (PronType=Dem, Prs) - mi libro and este libro hold a pronoun, el libro and cada libro do not, and neither do the quantifiers and the indefinites (cada, todos, ningún, otro, cualquier)
    Arguments NOUN, PROPN, PRON, without the determiners that count as pronouns
    Content words CONTENT_UD_POS of anyts.constants (NOUN, PROPN, ADJ, VERB, ADV) without the demonstratives
    Demonstratives PronType=Dem
    Tense and mood features Tense and Mood 
    from ests import CohesionStats

    "el problema. Es decir, la comisión aplazó la decisión. Por lo tanto, el "
    "problema sigue abierto."
)
cs = CohesionStats(text)
cs.print_stats()

    Metrics¶

    Every metric is an attribute of the object, computed when it is created, and get_stats gives the metrics of the table; aspect_repetition is an attribute outside it.

    Metric Measures Range Higher means
    noun_overlap_adjacent Share of the adjacent pairs of sentences sharing a noun 0-1, nan for one sentence Neighbouring sentences tied tighter
    noun_overlap_all Share of all the pairs of sentences sharing a noun 0-1, nan for one sentence The whole text tied tighter
    argument_overlap_adjacent Share of the adjacent pairs sharing a noun or a pronoun 0-1, nan for one sentence Neighbouring sentences tied tighter
    argument_overlap_all Share of all the pairs sharing a noun or a pronoun 0-1, nan for one sentence The whole text tied tighter
    content_overlap_adjacent Share of the adjacent pairs sharing a content word 0-1, nan for one sentence Neighbouring sentences tied tighter
    content_overlap_all Share of all the pairs sharing a content word 0-1, nan for one sentence The whole text tied tighter
    content_overlap_prop_adjacent Mean share of the shared content words (Dice) in the adjacent pairs 0-1, nan for one sentence More words shared by neighbours
    content_overlap_prop_all Mean share of the shared content words (Dice) in all the pairs 0-1, nan for one sentence More words shared across the text
    p_pronouns Share of the pronouns among the words 0-1 More reference by pronouns
    pronoun_noun_ratio Ratio of the pronouns to the nouns 0 and above, nan without nouns More pronouns per noun
    p_demonstratives Share of the demonstratives among the words 0-1 More pointing back
    p_given Share of the content words whose lemma was used before in the text 0-1, nan without content words More given information
    tense_repetition Share of the adjacent pairs of sentences with the same dominant tense 0-1, nan without two adjacent sentences with the feature A steadier tense
    mood_repetition Share of the adjacent pairs with the same dominant mood 0-1, nan without two adjacent sentences with the feature A steadier mood
    temporal_cohesion Mean of the repetitions of the tense and of the mood 0-1, nan when one of them is nan A steadier time frame
    connectors Connectors per 1000 words 0-1000 More explicit links
    connectors_inter Connectors linking a sentence to the ones before per 1000 words 0-1000 More links between sentences
    connectors_intra Connectors linking the parts of a sentence per 1000 words 0-1000 More links inside sentences
    connectors_<class> Connectors of a class per 1000 words: causal, adversative, concessive, temporal, additive, conditional, reformulative 0-1000 More links of the class
    connectors_primary, connectors_secondary Primary and secondary connectors per 1000 words 0-1000 More links of the kind

    The functions of the overlaps, the givenness and the repetitions (calc_overlap, calc_proportional_overlap, calc_overlaps, dice, count_given, calc_repetition, dominant) are those of anyts.cohesion_stats, which esTS does not re-export; they are on the anyTS page.

    Binary overlap¶

    noun_overlap_adjacent · noun_overlap_all · argument_overlap_adjacent · argument_overlap_all · content_overlap_adjacent · content_overlap_all · Coh-Metrix

    The binary overlap of Coh-Metrix (CRFNO1, CRFAO1, CRFSO1 over the adjacent pairs, CRFNOa, CRFAOa, CRFSOa over all of them): the share of the pairs of sentences that share the lemma of at least one noun, argument or content word; nan for a text shorter than two sentences.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of pairs sharing a lemma}}{\textrm{Number of pairs}} \]

    Proportional overlap¶

    content_overlap_prop_adjacent · content_overlap_prop_all · Coh-Metrix

    The proportional overlap of Coh-Metrix (CRFCWO1, CRFCWOa): the Dice coefficient of the sets of the lemmas of the content words \(A\) and \(B\) of two sentences, averaged over the adjacent pairs or over all of them, 0 for two sentences without content words; nan for a text shorter than two sentences.

    \[ \frac{1}{\textrm{Number of pairs}} \sum_{(A, B)} \frac{2 \times |A \cap B|}{|A| + |B|} \]

    Pronouns and demonstratives¶

    p_pronouns · pronoun_noun_ratio · p_demonstratives

    The pronouns, the nouns and the demonstratives are the words analyze marks so, with the features of word_info by default.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of pronouns}}{\textrm{Number of words}}, \quad \frac{\textrm{Number of pronouns}}{\textrm{Number of nouns}}, \quad \frac{\textrm{Number of demonstratives}}{\textrm{Number of words}} \]

    In esTS they are those of the table of the classes of words above, the features of token_info.

    Givenness¶

    p_given

    The share of the content words whose lemma was used before in the text, the current sentence included; the first occurrence of a lemma is new.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of given content words}}{\textrm{Number of content words}} \]

    Temporal cohesion¶

    tense_repetition · mood_repetition · temporal_cohesion · Coh-Metrix

    The repetition of the tense of Coh-Metrix (SMTEMP): every sentence gets the dominant value of the feature of its verbs - the most frequent one, the first of the equally frequent - and the metric is the share of the adjacent pairs of sentences with the same value; a pair where one of the sentences has no verb with the feature is skipped, and without a single such pair the value is nan.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of pairs with the same dominant value}}{\textrm{Number of pairs with the feature}} \]

    Spanish has no aspect in the Universal Dependencies annotation of the models, so esTS compares the mood next to the tense (temporal_features is ("tense", "mood")): temporal_cohesion is the mean of tense_repetition and mood_repetition. The attribute aspect_repetition of the core is nan without the feature Aspect.

    Connectors¶

    connectors · connectors_inter · connectors_intra · connectors_<class> · connectors_primary · connectors_secondary

    The occurrences of the connectors found in every sentence by the matcher of connector_matcher_class, per 1000 words. An inter-sentential connector links a sentence to the ones before: it opens a sentence, unless the matcher takes it for a subordinator (is_subordinator), whose clause or phrase belongs to the same sentence; the others are intra-sentential. Every class and kind of the matcher gives a density: connectors_causal, connectors_adversative, connectors_concessive, connectors_temporal, connectors_additive, connectors_conditional, connectors_reformulative, connectors_primary and connectors_secondary by default.

    \[ 1000 \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of connectors}}{\textrm{Number of words}} \]

    The subordinators of esTS are the one-word connectors tagged SCONJ or ADP (cuando, aunque, si) and the conjunctive and prepositional phrases of CONNECTOR_SUBORDINATORS (ya que, a pesar de, siempre que), but porque and conque, which open a sentence linking it to the one before (CONNECTOR_PARCELLATION: ¿Por qué? Porque...). In Ya que estás aquí, ayúdame the connector is intra-sentential; in No vino. Porque llovía. and Sin embargo, no. it is inter-sentential. The anaphoric forms of the prepositional subordinators (a pesar de esto, gracias a ello) are entries of the dictionary of their own, which link a sentence to the one before.

    load_connectors()

    The connector dictionary of esTS: the file ests/resources/connectors.tsv, 273 discourse markers (marcadores del discurso) of Martín Zorraquino and Portolés (1999) and conjunctions of the NGLE, each with its class of CONNECTOR_CLASSES and its kind of CONNECTOR_KINDS of anyts.constants. The classes are the causal, adversative, temporal and additive connectives of Coh-Metrix with the concessive, conditional and reformulative ones added. The dictionary is read-only and shared by every caller, so a dictionary of your own goes through the connectors parameter.

    Class Key Examples
    Causal causal porque, por lo tanto, así que
    Adversative adversative pero, sin embargo, en cambio
    Concessive concessive aunque, a pesar de, de todos modos
    Temporal temporal cuando, a continuación, por último
    Additive (ordinals included) additive y, además, por otra parte, en primer lugar
    Conditional conditional si, a menos que, de lo contrario
    Reformulative (operators of concretion included) reformulative es decir, en resumen, por ejemplo, en particular, sobre todo

    The primary connectors (primary) are conjunctions, conjunctive locutions and adverbs (porque, aunque, además), the secondary ones (secondary) lexicalized phrases (sin embargo, por lo tanto, es decir). si also opens indirect questions and exclamations (no sé si vendrá, ¡pero si es él!) - about a quarter of its occurrences in the corpus of literature - and siempre que is temporal with the indicative and conditional with the subjunctive; both are counted in their class of the dictionary. The rules by which a word of the dictionary counts as a connector are those of the matcher.

    from ests.cohesion_stats import load_connectors

connectors = load_connectors()
len(connectors), connectors["por lo tanto"]

    (273, ('causal', 'secondary'))

    Warning

    The words of a marker are counted as words like any other: the noun embargo of sin embargo is a noun and a content word of its sentence, so a text that repeats a marker gains a little overlap from it.

    Connector matcher¶

    SpanishConnectorMatcher(connectors)

    The search of the connectors (discourse markers: because, however, on the other hand) of a dictionary in the sentences of a text. The dictionary gives the class of every connector - causal, adversative, concessive, temporal, additive, conditional or reformulative (CONNECTOR_CLASSES) - and its kind, primary or secondary (CONNECTOR_KINDS). A connector is matched by the normalized forms of its words, the longest one at every position, without overlaps; a period or a hyphen inside a connector may be split off by the tokenizer (e.g. as e, g). An occurrence is a Connector: the number of the sentence sent, the positions start and end of its words, the connector text as the dictionary writes it, its class cls and kind kind.

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a dictionary that is not a mapping of a phrase to a pair of strings;
    • ParameterError - an unknown class or kind.

    SpanishConnectorMatcher is a subclass of the core ConnectorMatcher with the rules of Spanish; words and connectors are compared in lower case. A one-word connector takes a part of speech of CONNECTOR_POS - a conjunction, a particle, an adverb, an adposition, an interjection, a proper noun or X - or of CONNECTOR_POS_EXTRA for its form (pues and verbigracia as NOUN, resumiendo, recapitulando and concluyendo as VERB). It is no connector right after a determiner, so el antes y el después holds one connector, y, and not three; and as a proper noun it counts only at the start of a sentence, where the models read a marker so (Primeramente), so the surname in Habló con Juan Luego ayer is no connector. A marker that is part of another phrase is dropped: the words after it of CONNECTOR_BLOCKED_AFTER (antes de la reunión, en cuanto a, a pesar de todo lo que), the words before it of CONNECTOR_BLOCKED_BEFORE (algo así como) and the parts of speech of the word after it of CONNECTOR_BLOCKED_AFTER_POS (sobre todo el texto, a pesar de todo el esfuerzo) make it none, while antes, firmó el acta and sobre todo cuando llueve count their marker. A few markers depend on their place: pues opening a sentence opens a reply (Pues bien) and is no connector (CONNECTOR_NOT_AT_START), and encima counts inside a sentence only after y, e, ni or pero (y encima llueve, CONNECTOR_ONLY_AFTER). These lists are attributes of the matcher: CONNECTOR_POS is pos_tags, the others are named without CONNECTOR_ in lower case (CONNECTOR_BLOCKED_AFTER is blocked_after); not_after_pos is the determiner DET, start_only_pos the proper noun PROPN, and read_through_marks is empty in esTS. A punctuation mark inside a conjunctive phrase ending in que, como or si (CONNECTOR_SPLIT_BY_MARKS) makes the conjunction a word of its own, so No tire así, que se romperá and lo de siempre, que... hold no connector; the consecutive phrases with de modo, de manera, de forma or de suerte (de modo que, de tal manera que) and bien es verdad que are left out, as old texts put a comma before their que (de tal modo, que).

    find(words, sent_index, pos, breaks) returns the occurrences of the connectors in a sentence in the order of its words. The words are those of one sentence without punctuation, the first one its start. With the parts of speech, a one-word connector counts only with one of pos_tags or of pos_extra for its form, never after one of not_after_pos, and with one of start_only_pos only at the start of the sentence; a word without a part of speech (None) passes. Without the parts of speech the rules that read them are skipped. The positions of the punctuation marks breaks cut a connector off its neighbours: a rule does not read a word across a mark, so in so, the end the word after the comma neither blocks nor lets the connector count, unless the connector is one of read_through_marks, and a mark between the words of a connector of split_by_marks makes it none; without them the words are read as adjacent.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    words list[str] - Words of the sentence
    sent_index int 0 Number of the sentence, written into the occurrences
    pos list[str] None Parts of speech of the words
    breaks set[int] None Positions of the words a punctuation mark stands before

    Words that are not a list of strings and parts of speech that are not a list of strings and None (the tags token.pos_ of spaCy, not the numbers token.pos) and positions of the marks that are not a collection raise SourceTypeError, a number of the sentence or a position of a mark that is not an integer, a position of a mark that is not that of a word after the first and parts of speech not as many as the words ParameterError.

    CohesionStats passes the positions of the punctuation marks of every sentence, so in sin gracia alguna, y el amor the comma cuts y off the determiner alguna and it counts, and encima after a comma counts too.

    from ests.cohesion_stats import SpanishConnectorMatcher, load_connectors

matcher = SpanishConnectorMatcher(load_connectors())
words = ["Vivía", "sin", "gracia", "alguna", "y", "el", "amor", "llegó"]
pos = ["VERB", "ADP", "NOUN", "DET", "CCONJ", "DET", "NOUN", "VERB"]
matcher.find(words, pos=pos)
matcher.find(words, pos=pos, breaks={4})

    is_subordinator(connector, pos=None) checks whether an occurrence of a connector ties its clause or phrase to its own sentence: a connector of subordinators, or a one-word connector whose part of speech pos is SCONJ or ADP, but none of parcellation. The clause of a subordinating conjunction and the phrase of a preposition belong to the sentence of the connector wherever it stands, so a subordinator opening a sentence (when he came, ..., despite the rain, ...) does not link it to the ones before. A connector that is not a Connector raises SourceTypeError.

    subordinators is CONNECTOR_SUBORDINATORS and parcellation is CONNECTOR_PARCELLATION.

    matcher.is_subordinator(matcher.find(["Ya", "que", "estás", "aquí"])[0])
matcher.is_subordinator(matcher.find(["Porque", "llovía"])[0], "SCONJ")

    Connector search¶

    find_connectors(words, connectors=None, sent_index=0, pos=None, breaks=None)

    Searches a sentence for connectors with the find method of SpanishConnectorMatcher over the dictionary connectors, that of load_connectors by default; the other parameters and the errors are those of the matcher and of find. Without breaks, the positions of the words a punctuation mark stands before (find_marks of a string), the words are read as adjacent. With a dictionary of your own the function builds a matcher on every call, so over many sentences build SpanishConnectorMatcher(connectors) once and call its find.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    connectors dict[str, tuple[str, str]] None Class and kind by connector

find_connectors(["El", "antes", "y", "el", "después"], pos=["DET", "NOUN", "CCONJ", "DET", "NOUN"])
find_connectors(["No", "tire", "así", "que", "se", "romperá"], breaks={3})

    Word features¶

    token_info(token)

    The features of a spaCy token for the cohesion statistics by its Universal Dependencies annotation, after the table of the classes of words: a WordInfo named tuple with lemma (the lemma_ of the token in lower case), noun, pronoun, demonstrative, argument, content, tense and mood (the first value of Tense and of Mood, None without it). LexicalStats takes its content words from it.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - spaCy token 
    from ests.cohesion_stats import token_info
from ests.utils import get_nlp

doc = get_nlp()("Mi hermano miraba este libro.")
token_info(doc[2])
token_info(doc[3])
