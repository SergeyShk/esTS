    Text highlighting¶

    HighlightedText(source, layers=None, long_sent_word_factor=30, complex_syl_factor=4, stopwords=None, cliches=None, alliteration_threshold=0.001)

    Highlighting of a text by layers, in the manner of the style checkers: every layer marks the fragments of a text that a statistic counts - long sentences, complex words, the passive. The data source is a text or a Doc object of spaCy. The result shows in Jupyter as HTML with styles and a legend; to_html returns the markup, and highlights keeps the fragments for a rendering of one's own. Fragments of different layers may overlap.

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a source that is neither a string nor a Doc;
    • SourceError - a source without words;
    • ParameterError - layers that are not a name or a list of names, a layer that is unknown or one that needs an annotation the source lacks.
    highlight(source, layers=None, long_sent_word_factor=30, complex_syl_factor=4, stopwords=None, cliches=None, alliteration_threshold=0.001)

    The function highlight returns a HighlightedText object of esTS with the layers of the statistics of the library:

    Group Layer What it marks Statistic
    Readability long_sents Sentences of long_sent_word_factor words or more BasicStats
    complex_words Words of complex_syl_factor syllables or more BasicStats
    rare_words Words with a lemma beyond the embedded top 10000; numbers, words with a hyphen or a digit and stopwords are not highlighted LexicalStats
    Syntax passive Passive verb forms with their auxiliary ser or their se; the note tells the two apart and marks the passive with ser without an agent SyntaxStats
    participle_clauses Participial clauses SyntaxStats
    gerund_clauses Gerund clauses SyntaxStats
    de_chains Chains of complements with de with their head SyntaxStats
    split_predicates Split predicates from the verb to the noun SyntaxStats
    Officialese verbal_nouns Nouns derived from a verb StyleStats
    compound_prepositions Compound prepositions of COMPOUND_PREPOSITIONS StyleStats
    cliches Clichés of OFFICIALESE_CLICHES or of the parameter cliches StyleStats
    Style stopwords Stopwords of STOPWORDS or of the parameter stopwords, the water of the text StyleStats
    parentheticals Parenthetical expressions StyleStats
    connectors Discourse markers, the class and the kind in the note CohesionStats
    Phonics alliteration Repetitions of a consonant sound in neighbouring words, unlikely by the frequencies of the Spanish sounds; the note gives the sound and the letters that write it PhonStats

    The groups are defined in ests.constants.HIGHLIGHT_LAYER_GROUPS. The layers of the group "Syntax" need a Doc with a dependency parse and the lemmas (the models es_core_news_sm, es_core_news_md, es_core_news_lg), as SyntaxStats does; verbal_nouns needs a Doc with the parts of speech and the lemmas. The layers of HIGHLIGHT_DEFAULT_LAYERS the source allows are on by default - long sentences, complex words, passive, chains of de, split predicates, clichés; layers="all" turns on every layer allowed. The layers overlap (a compound preposition is made of stopwords), so pick the ones you need.

    A sentence is long from 30 words, the bound of the Spanish-language guides to plain language (the Comunidad de Madrid and the Gobierno de la Ciudad de Buenos Aires), and a word is complex from four syllables; complex_syl_factor=3, the bound of BasicStats, shows the words of n_complex_words.

    Alliteration

    An alliteration is looked for inside a sentence as a run of two neighbouring words or more with the same consonant sound in their stems. The sounds are the ones of the transcription, so casa and queso repeat k; the stem is the common start of the transcriptions of the word form and of its lemma (cantaban - k a n t a), which leaves out the endings that repeat by agreement (las casas blancas). The probability of a run under an independent spread of the sounds is the product over its words of the probability to meet the consonant among the sounds of the stem, \(1 - (1 - f)^n\), where \(f\) is the frequency of the sound in the corpus of literature and \(n\) the number of the sounds of the stem; a run is highlighted when the probability is below alliteration_threshold. A repetition of a rare sound shows in two or three words (deje la abeja), while the s of los suspiros se escapan is too frequent to count in two words. Words shorter than three letters (de, la, el), stopwords (que, los, con) and words without vowels neither break nor continue a run. The alliteration index of PhonStats measures how the repetitions cluster over the whole text; the highlighting shows where they are.

    The class extends the HighlightedText of the anyTS core with the hooks of Spanish from ests.constants: the layers with their names in the legend are HIGHLIGHT_LAYERS_DESC, the layers on by default HIGHLIGHT_DEFAULT_LAYERS, the annotations of a Doc a layer needs HIGHLIGHT_LAYER_ANNOTATIONS and the styles of the layers HIGHLIGHT_LAYER_STYLES; the prefix of the CSS classes is ests. The words of a string are those of WordsExtractor, without the punctuation, and its sentences those of SentsExtractor; a Doc without the sentence boundaries (a blank pipeline, a pipeline without the parser) is split into sentences by the rules of SentsExtractor too. A string with a character without a UTF-8 form raises SourceError from the Spanish tokenizer. The fragments of every layer are found by the finders of esTS.

    import spacy

    Parameters¶

    Parameter Type Default Description
    source str / Doc - Data source (a string or a Doc object)
    layers list[str] / str None Layers of the highlighting; if not given, the default layers the source allows; "all" - every layer allowed

    The parameters of the layers of esTS:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    long_sent_word_factor int 30 Minimum number of words of a long sentence
    complex_syl_factor int 4 Minimum number of syllables of a complex word
    stopwords list[str] / set[str] None List or set of stopwords; if not given, STOPWORDS and the one-word parenthetical expressions
    cliches list[str] / set[str] None List or set of clichés; if not given, OFFICIALESE_CLICHES
    alliteration_threshold float 0.001 Probability of a repetition of a consonant under an independent spread of the sounds, below which the repetition is alliteration

    A number of words or syllables that is not an integer of at least one or a threshold outside (0, 1] raises ParameterError, stopwords or clichés that are not a list or a set of strings SourceTypeError.

    Attributes¶

    Attribute Type Description
    text str Text of the data source
    layers tuple[str] Layers turned on, in the order of drawing
    highlights tuple[Highlight] Highlighted fragments, sorted by their start and then by descending end
    counts dict[str, int] Number of fragments of every layer 
    Highlight(start, end, layer, note='')

    A fragment of the highlighting: an immutable object with the parameters as its fields.

    Field Type Default Description
    start int - Position of the first character of the fragment in the text
    end int - Position after the last character of the fragment
    layer str - Layer of the highlighting
    note str '' Explanation of the fragment for the tooltip

    The notes of esTS are in English: they give the number of words of the sentence or of the clause, of syllables of the word, the length of the chain, the phrase of the list, the class and the kind of the connector, the sound of the alliteration and its letters.

    Methods¶

    to_html(legend=True, css=True) returns the HTML markup of the highlighted text: a div of the class <prefix>-highlight with the legend, its counts and the text, where a highlighted segment is a span of the classes <prefix>-hl and <prefix>-hl-<layer> with the notes in its title; <prefix> is css_prefix. Overlapping fragments of different layers give segments with several classes, in the order of drawing. Line breaks (\n, \r\n, \r) become character references, one per break, so the markup can go into Markdown.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    legend bool True Add the legend with the counts of the fragments
    css bool True Add the styles of the layers

    The class method css() returns the styles to_html adds: the container, the legend and the text under the classes of css_prefix, the declarations of layer_styles for every layer, and a dark colour of the text on the layers with a background.

    from ests.visualizers import highlight

poem = "El cierzo corre por el campo yerto alborotando en blancos torbellinos la nieve silenciosa."
highlight(poem, layers="alliteration").to_html(legend=False, css=False)

    '<div class="ests-highlight"><div class="ests-highlight-text">El cierzo corre por el campo yerto <span class="ests-hl ests-hl-alliteration" title="alliteration on /b/ (b, v)">alborotando en blancos torbellinos la nieve</span> silenciosa.</div></div>'

    Helpers of the layers¶

    The words and the sentences the finders of the layers take; the helpers are in ests.visualizers.highlight.

    Word(start, end, text, pos=None, lemma=None)

    A word with its position in the text and, when known, its part of speech and lemma.

    Sent(start, end, n_words)

    A sentence with its position and number of words.

    group_words_by_sents(words, sents)

    The words of every sentence; the words out of the sentences are skipped.

    Finders of the layers¶

    The fragments of every layer are found by a function of ests.visualizers.highlight that returns a list of Highlight: from the words and the sentences of the text, or from the Doc for the syntactic layers (SYNTAX_FINDERS gives them by layer). All but the passive, the participial and gerund clauses and the chains of de are the finders of the core with the hooks of Spanish.

    Long sentences and complex words¶

    find_long_sents(sents, long_sent_word_factor)

    The layer long_sents: the sentences of long_sent_word_factor words or more, the note with their number of words.

    find_complex_words(words, complex_syl_factor)

    The layer complex_words: the words of complex_syl_factor syllables or more by count_syllables, the note with their number of syllables.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    sents list[Sent] - Sentences with their positions and numbers of words
    long_sent_word_factor int - Minimum number of words of a long sentence
    words list[Word] - Words with their positions
    complex_syl_factor int - Minimum number of syllables of a complex word

    Raises:

    • ParameterError - a number of words or syllables that is not an integer of at least one.

text = "El felinólogo examinó al gato. Lo devolvieron."
words = [Word(*word) for word in iter_text_words(text)]
find_long_sents([Sent(0, 30, 5), Sent(31, 46, 2)], 5)
find_complex_words(words, 4)

    Single words¶

    find_rare_words(words)

    The layer rare_words: the words whose key (lemma_key) is beyond the 10,000 most frequent lemmas (get_rank), as p_beyond_top10000 of LexicalStats counts them; a word tagged PROPN keeps its form. Only the words of two Spanish letters or more that are not stopwords are looked at.

    find_stopwords(words, stopwords=None)

    The layer stopwords: the words of stopwords in any case or, without the list, the stopwords of is_stopword.

    find_verbal_nouns(words)

    The layer verbal_nouns: the words tagged NOUN whose lemma is derived from a verb, as for the verbal nouns of StyleStats.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    words list[Word] - Words with their positions; with their parts of speech and lemmas for find_verbal_nouns
    stopwords list[str] / set[str] None List or set of stopwords; is_stopword if not given

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - stopwords that are not a list or a set of strings.
    from ests.utils import iter_text_words
from ests.visualizers.highlight import Word, find_rare_words, find_stopwords

text = "El felinólogo examinó minuciosamente al gato."
words = [Word(*word) for word in iter_text_words(text)]
find_rare_words(words)
find_stopwords(words)

    Phrases¶

    find_phrase_highlights(words, phrases, layer, label)

    The fragments of the phrases of a list, matched with their contractions and the forms of their verbs (expand_phrases); the note gives the label and the phrase of the list (cliché: «proceder a»).

    find_compound_prepositions(words)

    The layer compound_prepositions: the phrases of COMPOUND_PREPOSITIONS.

    find_cliches(words, cliches=None)

    The layer cliches: the phrases of cliches, of OFFICIALESE_CLICHES if not given.

    find_parentheticals(words)

    The layer parentheticals: the phrases of PARENTHETICALS.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    words list[Word] - Words with their positions
    phrases list[str] / set[str] - Phrases, words separated by spaces
    layer str - Layer of the highlighting
    label str - Label of the fragment in the note
    cliches list[str] / set[str] None List or set of clichés

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - phrases or clichés that are not a list or a set of strings.

from ests.visualizers.highlight import Word, find_cliches, find_compound_prepositions

    Connectors¶

    find_connector_highlights(words, sents, text=None)

    The layer connectors: the connectors of CohesionStats inside every sentence, found by its matcher SpanishConnectorMatcher, a one-word connector checked by its part of speech when the words have it; the note gives the connector, its class and its kind. With the text, the rules of the matcher read no word across a punctuation mark between two words, so así, que is no connector; HighlightedText passes the text of its source.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    words list[Word] - Words with their positions and parts of speech
    sents list[Sent] - Sentences with their positions
    text str None Text of the words, for the punctuation marks between them

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a text that is not a string.
    from ests.utils import iter_text_words
from ests.visualizers.highlight import Sent, Word, find_connector_highlights

text = "Sin embargo, el plan no se aprobó."
words = [Word(*word) for word in iter_text_words(text)]
find_connector_highlights(words, [Sent(0, len(text), len(words))], text)

    [Highlight(start=0, end=11, layer='connectors', note='connector «sin embargo»: adversative, secondary')]

    Alliteration¶

    get_stem_sounds(word)

    The sounds of the stem of a word form: the common start of the transcriptions of the form and of its lemma (cantaban - k a n t a, cogió - k o x). When it is shorter than two sounds, as for the suppletive forms (fue - ser), the stem is the whole form.

    calc_alliteration_runs(text, threshold=0.001)

    The runs of calc_alliteration_runs of the core over the stems of get_stem_sounds and the consonant sounds of ALLITERATION_FREQUENCIES, their frequencies in SOUND_FREQUENCIES; the words shorter than three letters, the stopwords and the words without vowels neither break nor continue a run. A run is a triple of the index of its first word, the index after its last word and its sound.

    find_alliteration(words, threshold=0.001, sents=None)

    The layer alliteration: the runs of calc_alliteration_runs inside every sentence, in the whole text without the sentences; the note gives the sound and the letters that write it (alliteration on /b/ (b, v)).

    Parameter Type Default Description
    word str - Word form in lower case
    text list[str] - List of words
    threshold float 0.001 Probability threshold (ALLITERATION_THRESHOLD of anyts.constants)
    words list[Word] - Words with their positions
    sents list[Sent] None Sentences with their positions

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a word that is not a string, words of calc_alliteration_runs that are not a list of strings;
    • ParameterError - a threshold outside (0, 1].
    from ests.visualizers.highlight import calc_alliteration_runs, get_stem_sounds

get_stem_sounds("cantaban"), get_stem_sounds("fue")
calc_alliteration_runs(["deje", "la", "abeja"])
calc_alliteration_runs(["los", "suspiros", "se", "escapan"])

[(0, 3, 'x')]

    Syntactic layers¶

    find_passive(doc)

    The layer passive: every passive form of is_passive with its auxiliary ser (fue construida) or its se (se construyó); the note tells the passive with se from the one with ser and marks a passive with ser without an agent (is_agentless).

    find_participle_clauses(doc)

    The layer participle_clauses: the subtree of every head of a participial clause, the note with its number of words.

    find_gerund_clauses(doc)

    The layer gerund_clauses: the subtree of every head of a gerund clause, the note with its number of words.

    find_de_chains(doc)

    The layer de_chains: the chains of de of two complements or more, a fragment from the head to the last complement (el aumento de la eficiencia del uso de los recursos), the note with the length of the chain.

    find_split_predicate_highlights(doc)

    The layer split_predicates: the pairs of a light verb and a noun of find_split_predicates, a fragment from the first word of the pair to the last (hacer una revisión), the note with their lemmas.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    doc Doc - Doc object with a dependency parse and the lemmas 
    from ests.utils import get_nlp
from ests.visualizers.highlight import (
    find_de_chains,
    find_gerund_clauses,
    find_participle_clauses,
    find_passive,
    find_split_predicate_highlights,
)

doc = get_nlp()(
    "El informe, redactado por el comité, fue aprobado tras hacer una revisión, "
    "siguiendo las normas del uso de los recursos."
)
find_passive(doc)
find_participle_clauses(doc)
find_gerund_clauses(doc)
find_de_chains(doc)
find_split_predicate_highlights(doc)
